# Scenario · Paper — QR / barcode / plaintext print

**Channel profile:** ~1–3 kB per print · hours–years · zero electromagnetic waves.

**Dual-pillar mode:** **archival** — the seal is fully verifiable from paper.

## How it works

Print a 128-byte envelope as a QR code. Any phone camera recovers the bytes; verification is local computation. No radio, no network, no electricity on the channel side — the pillars run on paper. The ultimate offline proof, and the cheapest demo in the project.

## Honest limits

Paper is copiable by anyone — which is fine: the envelope is a public claim whose authenticity is the signature, not the secrecy of the medium.
