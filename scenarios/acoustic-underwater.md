# Scenario · Underwater acoustics — radio dies here

**Channel profile:** B/s · seconds–minutes · EM waves are dead in water.

**Dual-pillar mode:** **lease residual → archival** — acoustic modems move one envelope per frame; verification is offline at both ends.

## How it works

Radio (and therefore IP/DNS) is dead underwater; submarines talk VLF/ELF at a few bytes per minute and sonar modems carry hundreds of bytes per frame. A 128-byte envelope fits one acoustic frame. The dual pillars work where the Internet has never worked.

## Honest limits

Sound is slow (1.5 km/s) and multipath-heavy. Latency measured in seconds; long deployments measure the lease in residual form.
