# Scenario · NFC / RFID — the contact moment

**Channel profile:** kB per tap · contact/near-field · one direction dominant.

**Dual-pillar mode:** **live lease at the moment of contact.**

## How it works

Tap-to-verify: an envelope passes at the contact instant and is verified on the spot, offline. Machine-readable work orders, tool identity, spare-part provenance — every physical touchpoint becomes a verification point.

## Honest limits

Range is centimeters by design. After the tap, the envelope ages (residual/archival) until the next contact.
