# Scenario · Fiber & cable — the current landlord

**Channel profile:** Gb/s · ms latency · bidirectional · dense coverage.

**Dual-pillar mode:** **live lease** — the full protocol at native speed.

## How it works

The cheapest host today. Every mechanism runs unmodified: derive, dispatch, seal, verify, lease renewal every 120 s. All published demos and the conformance challenge assume this channel.

## Honest limits

None technical. The dependence itself is the design constraint the other scenarios remove: the moment fiber disappears, the dual pillars do not notice.
