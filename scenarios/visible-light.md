# Scenario · Visible light — LiFi / IR beacons

**Channel profile:** kb/s–Mb/s · ms · line of sight.

**Dual-pillar mode:** **live lease** where line of sight holds.

## How it works

A beacon blinking a standing envelope is a lighthouse for trust: receivers verify offline at full rigor. Indoor industrial cells, vehicular headlight links, and aircraft cabin scenarios fit the same shape.

## Honest limits

Line of sight and ambient light are the constraints; mobility beyond the beam degrades to the last received envelope (residual).
