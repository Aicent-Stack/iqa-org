# Scenario · ISM free-band radio — LoRa & friends (the aerial root)

**Channel profile:** B/s–kb/s · seconds · broadcast-capable · license-free low power.

**Dual-pillar mode:** **live lease, broadcast form** — a beacon re-announces current standing envelopes every 120 s; receivers verify offline.

## How it works

One AE-128 envelope fits a single LoRa frame. No carrier, no subscription: the gas root that grows past the epibiotic host. A base station broadcasting current standing envelopes turns the lease TTL into the broadcast cadence.

## Honest limits

Power limits and duty-cycle regulations apply per region. Return channels (if needed) are slower than the broadcast; interactive flows degrade to uplink-request/broadcast-answer.
