# Scenario · Postal / courier / carrier pigeon — the slowest valid channel

**Channel profile:** days · physical transport · RFC 1149 approved (as a joke, then implemented).

**Dual-pillar mode:** **archival** — days-old envelopes verify perfectly; the bytes say how old they are.

## How it works

A paper envelope in an envelope: the outer one is postal, the inner one is cryptographic. Standings, seals, and full evidence chains travel by mail where no network exists. RFC 1149 (IP over avian carriers) was implemented in 2001 with nine pigeons; a 128-byte envelope is a lighter payload than an IP packet.

## Honest limits

Latency is the lease’s enemy: days-old standing is stated as such by the verification output. For provenance and audit copies, latency does not matter at all — the seal carries its own date.
