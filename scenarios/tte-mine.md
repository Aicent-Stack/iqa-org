# Scenario · Through-the-earth — mines and tunnels

**Channel profile:** bytes per minute · magnetic induction / leaky feeder · EM waves die in rock.

**Dual-pillar mode:** **archival + lease residual** — half an envelope is sometimes enough.

## How it works

IP/DNS are dead at the mine entrance. A TTE link carrying even an AID fragment lets a receiver confirm a seal’s subject against a carried envelope; a robot that physically carries its envelope out of the mine completes verification in full at the surface. The worse the channel, the larger the dual pillars’ relative advantage — nothing else survives here at all.

## Honest limits

Bitrates forbid interactive flows. The honest promise: last known standing, verifiable whenever bytes arrive — however slowly, however indirectly.
